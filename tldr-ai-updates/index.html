TLDR AI Updates Uploader

A personal, open-source tool that turns the day's top technology stories from the TLDR newsletters into short explainer videos and posts them to its owner's YouTube channel, TLDR AI Updates. It runs on the owner's computer once a day and is not offered to other users.

How it works

  1. Reads the public TLDR newsletter pages and selects the most notable technology stories.
  2. Writes a short script for each video, with links to the original sources.
  3. Generates narration with a local text-to-speech voice and renders a 40–45 second vertical video with captions and an animated presenter.
  4. Uploads each video to the owner's channel through the YouTube Data API, with a title, description, source links and tags, and sets its visibility.

YouTube API usage

The tool only acts on the owner's own channel: it uploads videos the owner produced, reads the channel name to confirm the connection, and changes the visibility of videos it uploaded. It reads no data about viewers or other channels.
